Study Resources & Guide

Below are some tips, subject resources, and exam prep guides to help you study better.

📝 Top Study Tips

  • Use the Pomodoro Technique — study 25 minutes, break 5 minutes.
  • Summarize notes in your own words after every lecture.
  • Teach the topic to someone else to test your understanding.
  • Avoid multitasking — focus on one subject at a time.
  • Sleep at least 7-8 hours before an exam day.

📚 Subject-wise Resources

  • Mathematics: Khan Academy, PatrickJMT videos
  • Computer Science: freeCodeCamp, MDN Web Docs
  • English: Grammarly Blog, Purdue OWL Writing Lab
  • Science: CrashCourse YouTube channel

🎯 Exam Prep Guide

  1. Make a revision timetable at least 2 weeks before exams.
  2. Practice past papers under timed conditions.
  3. Revise weak topics first, strong topics last.
  4. Stay calm and take short breaks to avoid burnout.
